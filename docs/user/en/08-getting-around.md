# Getting around Muninn

> **Demo mode:** if you come in without an account, what you create is only kept in memory and disappears when you reload the
> page. To keep it, [sign in](02-installation.md#signing-in).

## On your computer: the sidebar

On the left you have the sections of the modules you have turned on (see below): **Home**, **Projects**, **Notes**,
**Finance**, **Assistant**, **News** and **GitHub**.

Under **Projects** you'll see your projects with their logo or, if they don't have one, their initial and color. Click one to open its board, or the
**+** button to create another.

At the bottom of the bar is your account, with your name and photo (or your initials). Click it (or press **⌘⇧U** on Mac, **Ctrl ⇧ U** on
Windows and Linux) and a menu opens with **Account**, **Settings**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Documentation**, **About Muninn** and
**Sign out**. Move through it with the arrow keys and close it with **Esc**.

Want more room? The button next to the logo, at the top, **collapses the bar** down to just the icons (like on a
tablet). Press it again to expand it. Muninn remembers it on this computer.

## On a tablet: the icon bar

On a medium-sized screen, like an iPad held upright, the sidebar shrinks to a narrow column with just the
icons, to leave more room for what you're doing. Hold your finger or the mouse over an icon to see its name.
Your projects don't show up here: you'll find them in **Projects**. If you turn the iPad or use a wider screen, the full
bar comes back.

## On your phone: the tabs and your account

On your phone, at the bottom you have up to four tabs, each with its name. At first they're **Home**, **Projects**,
**Finance** and **Notes** (tabs for a turned-off module don't show up), but **you choose them** (see below). The one in color
is where you are.

As you scroll down a screen, the bar shrinks and leaves only the tab you're on, so you can see more. Scroll up a little,
or tap it, and it comes back in full.

On the right there's always **your photo** (or your initials). Tap it and the **Your account** sheet slides up from the bottom, with everything else:

- At the top, **your account**: tap it to see how you sign in, your sessions and two-factor authentication, and **Sign out** at the end.
- **Go to**: the sections you don't have in the bar (Notes, News, GitHub, Docs…) and **Search**. The **Assistant** shows up
  dimmed: for now it answers on your computer, with its model; it'll come to your phone later.
- **Settings**, in groups, like on your computer.
- **Documentation** and **About Muninn**.

Each setting opens inside the same sheet; **‹** takes you back to the list. To close it, tap outside, drag the little bar at the
top down or press the **X**: you stay on the screen you were on. If you open a link to a setting (for example, the
"Go to Modules" button), the sheet opens right on that setting, on top of what you were looking at.

All the sheets that slide up from the bottom (a new transaction, filters, changing a date…) close the same way: drag the
little bar at the top down, tap outside or press the **X**. If you let go before pulling it down far enough, it goes back to its place.

### Choose the bottom tabs

In **Settings › Bottom bar** (in your account sheet) you choose which tabs you want and in what order:

- At the top you see how the bar will look.
- In **In the bar**, the arrows move a tab and the switch removes it. There's always at least one left.
- In **Not in the bar**, the switch adds it. **Four** fit: to add another, remove one first.
- Besides the sections, you can add **Search**, which opens search without leaving where you are.
- You can't add the **Assistant** yet, because it doesn't answer on your phone yet.

Each change applies right away and can be undone from the notification. With an account, it's saved to it: your bar follows you to all
your devices. If you turn off a module, its tab is hidden, and it comes back when you turn it on.

### Links that leave Muninn

On your phone (and on any touch screen), when you tap a link to another website, for example in a description, a note or the
title of a news article, you don't leave Muninn right away: a small menu pops up next to the link saying where it goes, with
**Open link** and **Copy link**. Tap outside to close it. That way, an accidental tap doesn't take you out of the app. Buttons
that already say they open outside, like **Open** on a news article, open directly. On your computer, clicking opens the link
as usual.

## Home

The first screen sums up your day: at the top, the date and a line with what matters ("Nothing due today · 4 in progress"),
and a strip with four numbers: **Due today**, **In progress**, **Overdue** and **Done · 7 days**. Below that, your overdue tasks,
today's and the ones you've completed today (if there are none, you'll see "All clear" and what your next task is); what
you have **in progress**; what's due in the **coming days** and you haven't started yet (what's already in progress isn't repeated);
each **project** with its progress ("3 of 8") and a chart of the **last 7 days**. With Finance turned on, you also see
the **Upcoming charges** for your subscriptions. Tick a
task's checkbox to complete it; tick it again to reopen it. If something doesn't apply, you'll see a message in its place (for example,
"Nothing is due today or overdue."), not a gap.

If you don't have any projects or tasks yet, Home tells you so and offers to create the first one.

## Documentation

In **Documentation** (in your account menu, at the bottom of the sidebar, or in your account sheet on your phone) you'll find these same guides inside the app. On the
left you see the menu, grouped into **Getting started**, **Your work**, **Modules**, **Assistants and AI** and **Account**, with a
**Partial** or **Coming soon** badge on the ones that aren't complete yet; at the top, a search box
that looks through the titles and text of all of them. Each guide has its own link, an **On this page** index (on wide
screens) and the **Previous** and **Next** buttons. On your computer those two buttons always stay at the bottom, in view:
you don't need to scroll to the end to move on to the next guide.

## Turn modules on and off

In **Settings › Modules** you choose what you use: **Tasks and projects**, **Notes**, **Finance**, **GitHub** and **News**. What
you turn off disappears from the sidebar, the phone tabs, **Go to** in your account sheet and search, and the **N** key stops
opening "New task" if you turn off tasks. **Nothing is deleted**: when you turn it back on, everything is where it was. If you open a
link to something that's turned off, Muninn tells you and lets you turn it on right there. Each change lets you know and can be undone.

By default only **Tasks and projects** and **Notes** are on: you can turn on Finance, GitHub and News whenever you want.
If you go into one that's turned off (with an old link, for example), you'll see "Finance is turned off", with the button to
turn it on or go to Modules. **Focus** can't be turned on yet. AI assistants connected
through MCP still see all your data even if the app hides it.

## Search or go anywhere

Press **⌘K** on Mac or **Ctrl K** on Windows and Linux (or the **Search or ask…** button in the
sidebar) and type:

- The name of a section or a project, to go straight there. Accents and capitals don't matter.
- Words from the title or text of a **task** (it shows up with its project) or a **note**, in any order, to
  open it. It searches from two letters on.
- An action, like "new project", "new note", "glass" or "sign out".
- Anything else, to ask Muninn: the [Assistant](11-assistant.md) opens with your question already sent
  (if the model on your computer is ready; if not, it leaves it typed in).
- With News turned on, also **Search News** for what you typed.

Move with the arrow keys, open with **Enter** and close with **Esc**.

## Create a project

1. Press **New project** in Home or in Projects, the **+** in the sidebar, or search for "new project".
2. Type the name and choose a color.
3. Press **Create project**. Its board opens with five columns.

If you made a mistake, press **Undo** in the notification that appears at the top: the project is archived.

## Create a task

Press **New task** (or the **N** key), choose the project and column, type the title and press **Create task**. More
details in [Tasks](04-tasks.md).

## Archive a project

On the board, open the **⋯** menu and choose **Archive project**. It disappears from the lists, but it isn't
deleted. Press **Undo** in the notification to get it back.

## Change the appearance

In **Settings › Appearance**:

- **Language:** Spanish or English. In **Automatic**, Muninn uses your browser's language (and, if it's neither of
  the two, English).
- **Glass:** turns the translucent blur on panels and menus on or off. If your system has
  "Reduce transparency" turned on, Muninn starts without glass.
- **Accent color:** amber, blue, violet, teal or pink. It changes the buttons, the selection and the
  active tab in the bottom bar on your phone.

Each change applies right away and you can undo it from the notification. With an account, it's saved to it and
follows you to all your devices; in demo mode, only on this one.

> **Status:** the whole app and these guides are in English and Spanish. But
> what Muninn creates for you when you start (the board columns, the Finance categories) comes out in Spanish even if you use
> English: you can rename it.

> **Coming soon:** light theme.

## Settings

In **Settings** (in your account menu, at the bottom of the sidebar, or in your account sheet on your phone) the sections
are in groups. Next to each one you see
the most useful thing without opening it: how you sign in, how many modules you have on or what time the report runs.

| Group | Section | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| You | **Account** | Your name and email, how you sign in (code or GitHub) and **Sign out**. In demo mode, **Leave demo mode** |
| You | **Appearance** | Language, glass and accent color (see above) |
| You | **Bottom bar** | The phone tabs and their order (see [Choose the bottom tabs](#choose-the-bottom-tabs)) |
| You | **Keyboard shortcuts** | Shortcuts for search, the board and the whole app |
| Workspace | **Modules** | Turn modules on and off (see above) |
| Workspace | **Notifications** | Turn on notifications on this device, **Send test** and the devices that get them (see [Notifications](13-notifications.md)) |
| Assistant | **Local model** | Where Ollama is, which model the [Assistant](11-assistant.md) uses and **Test connection** |
| Assistant | **Morning report** | Turn it on, the time and **Make it now** (see [Assistant](11-assistant.md#morning-report)) |
| Integrations | **GitHub** | Each project's repository and the sync status (see [GitHub](09-github.md)) |
| Data | **Data and sync** | Where your data is stored, how it moves between devices and what happens offline (see [Your data](07-data-and-privacy.md)) |

At the bottom, **About Muninn** tells you which version you have and links to this documentation and to the code.

### Search for a setting

At the top of the menu there's a search box (press **/** to jump to it). Type, for example, "time" or "glass": the matching settings
show up, grouped by section. Pick one (with the mouse, or with **↑ ↓** and **Enter**) and Muninn takes you to that section and
highlights it for a moment. **Esc** clears the search.

### What you've changed

Some settings have a default value: glass, accent color and the morning report (off, at 08:30).
When one isn't at that value, it has a **dot** next to it, and so does its section in the menu. Next to the setting there's a button
with an arrow to reset it to its default, and at the top of the section, **Reset section** to reset them all. As
always, the notification lets you **Undo**.

Modules, the Ollama address and the model don't get a dot: they're about your setup, not preferences with a "right" value.

On very wide screens, to the right of each section you'll see the guide from this documentation that explains it.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⌘K** / **Ctrl K** | Opens or closes search |
| **⌘⇧U** / **Ctrl ⇧ U** | Opens your account menu, at the bottom of the sidebar |
| **N** | New task (outside a text field) |
| **↑** **↓** | Move through the search results |
| **Enter** | Open the selected result |
| **Esc** | Close search, a dialog, a menu or a task's panel; cancel a move |
| **Space** | With a card or column focused, pick it up and drop it |
| **← ↑ → ↓** | With something picked up, move it |

With the keyboard, the first **Tab** on each page offers you **Skip to content**.
