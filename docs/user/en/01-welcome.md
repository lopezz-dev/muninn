# Welcome to Muninn

Muninn is your **personal operating system**: one place for your projects, tasks, notes,
finances and the news you follow. On top of that, an AI assistant knows all that context and can tell you how you're doing.

The name comes from Norse mythology: Muninn (memory) was one of Odin's two ravens, which
flew across the world every day and came back to tell him what they had seen. Muninn remembers for you.

## How it's organized

Muninn is made of **modules** you can turn on or off depending on what you need:

| Module | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Tasks** | Projects with their own Kanban board, fully customizable |
| **Notes** | Markdown notes, on their own or linked to a project or task |
| **GitHub** | The issues from your repositories become tasks in the matching project |
| **Finance** | Accounts, expenses, income, transfers and subscriptions |
| **News** | Your blogs and websites via RSS, with a summary of what matters |
| **Focus** | A timer for deep work blocks *(coming soon)* |
| **[Assistant](11-assistant.md)** | You ask Muninn questions and ask it to do things, with a model that runs on your computer |
| **[Morning report](11-assistant.md#morning-report)** | If you turn it on, every day a note with what's on your plate today, upcoming charges and the news summary |

The Assistant and the morning report aren't modules: they're always there, whatever you turn on. And if you prefer Claude or another
assistant, you can [connect it to your data](06-ai-assistants.md).

## Where you can use it

- **Desktop:** Windows, macOS and Linux.
- **Browser:** at [muninn-sepia.vercel.app](https://muninn-sepia.vercel.app), with nothing to install.
- **Phone:** as an app installed from the browser (iPhone and Android).
- Everything **syncs** across your devices.

You can read these guides without an account, on the web: share the link to any of them with whoever you like.
